# HireAId

**Your AI recruiting team, on call 24/7.**

HireAId replaces the most exhausting part of hiring — chasing candidates on the phone — with an AI voice recruiter that screens, qualifies, and shortlists candidates for you, at a scale no human team can match. Post a role, and HireAId takes it from job description to a ranked shortlist of screened, interview-ready candidates, automatically.

## Why recruiters love HireAId

### 🎙️ An AI recruiter that actually calls candidates
No more chasing candidates through email and missed calls. HireAId's voice AI dials candidates directly, introduces your company, runs a natural, conversational screening interview, asks your custom qualifying questions, and handles objections — all without a human recruiter lifting the phone.

### ⚡ From job description to shortlist in minutes
Paste in a job description and HireAId instantly extracts the must-have skills, seniority level, and hiring criteria — no manual tagging required. It then sources matching candidates, ranks them against your role, and lines them up in a ready-to-call pipeline.

### 🧠 Every candidate is scored, not just logged
After every call, HireAId automatically generates a full scorecard: fit score, strengths, gaps, a plain-English summary, and a clear recommendation — Advance, Hold, or Reject. No more listening to call recordings just to know who's worth a second look.

### 🔁 Smart, human-like follow-up
Candidates don't always pick up on the first try. HireAId knows the difference between someone who missed the call and someone who didn't clear the bar — and gives your team the right next action every time: recall, give another chance, or move them straight to the next round.

### 🧪 Rehearse your screening before it ever reaches a real candidate
The Digital Twin Lab lets you test-drive your AI recruiter against realistic simulated candidates — including tough, skeptical, or evasive personas — so you can perfect your screening flow before a single real candidate ever hears it.

### 🪜 Multi-round pipelines that carry context forward
Design multi-stage interview pipelines — screening, technical, culture-fit, whatever your process needs — with each round's AI voice agent carrying forward context from earlier conversations, so candidates never have to repeat themselves.

### 📊 A real-time view of your entire funnel
See every open role, every candidate, every call outcome, and every pipeline stage from one clean dashboard. Know exactly who's been called, who's shortlisted, and who needs your attention — without digging through spreadsheets or inboxes.

### 🔒 Built for how recruiting teams actually work
Manage requisitions, candidate pipelines, call scripts, and screening outcomes as a team, with a shared view of every role's progress from sourcing to offer.

## Who it's for

- **Startups and growing teams** who need to screen a high volume of applicants without hiring a screening team.
- **Talent acquisition teams** who want every candidate interaction to be consistent, on-brand, and fully logged.
- **Recruiters** who are tired of playing phone tag and want their pipeline to move itself forward.

## The HireAId experience

1. **Post a role.** Describe the job — HireAId figures out the hiring criteria.
2. **Source and rank.** Candidates are found, scored, and added to your pipeline automatically.
3. **Let AI make the calls.** Your voice recruiter screens every candidate the same way, every time.
4. **Review the results.** Scorecards, transcripts, and recommendations are ready the moment the call ends.
5. **Move the best forward.** Advance top candidates to the next round with one click — memory and all.
